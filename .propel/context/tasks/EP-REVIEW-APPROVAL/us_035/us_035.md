US_035
## Story Title
Approve Related Records in Bulk
## Status
- **State**: Draft
- **Superseded By**: N/A
- **Change Request**: N/A
## Description
As compliance staff, I want to approve eligible related records in bulk so that routine review is efficient without bypassing controls.
## Acceptance Criteria
### AC-001: Apply bulk approval
- **Given**: Multiple selected records each meeting approval prerequisites
- **When**: staff submits bulk approval
- **Then**: each approved record has its own reviewer and timestamp audit entry
## Edge Cases
- Ineligible records are reported individually and are not approved by the bulk action.
