## Story Title
Display Field Review History
## Status

- **Superseded By**: N/A

## Description
As compliance staff, I want to see attributable field revisions so that I can audit review decisions.
## Acceptance Criteria
### AC-001: Show field history
- **Given**: A reviewed field
- **When**: staff opens its history
- **Then**: it displays AI or staff attribution, reviewer, timestamp, content change, and approval status
## Edge Cases
- A request without review permission does not expose content or reviewer identity.
