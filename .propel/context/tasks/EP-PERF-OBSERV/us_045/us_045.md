Monitor Service Availability

## Description
As a platform owner, I want service health and availability monitored so that the review workflow meets its production target.
## Acceptance Criteria
### AC-001: Alert on availability risk
- **Given**: deployed API and worker revisions
- **When**: health or availability thresholds are breached
- **Then**: monitoring emits an actionable alert and reports monthly API availability against the 99.9% target
## Edge Cases
- Scheduled maintenance announced at least 24 hours ahead is excluded from the availability calculation.
